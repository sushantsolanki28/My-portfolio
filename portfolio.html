About

I'm a Computer Science & Data Science student who genuinely enjoys the unglamorous part of analytics — cleaning duplicate rows, chasing down weird outliers, and figuring out why a metric looks off before anyone else notices.

Most of my practice comes from playing analyst on real-feeling datasets: card-member spending, restaurant orders, retail sales. I write the SQL, build the dashboard, and try to leave behind an answer a stakeholder can act on — not just a chart.

Next up: teaching myself the machine-learning side (regression, clustering, decision trees) and poking at what GenAI can do for faster insight generation.
